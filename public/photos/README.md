## A fourth photograph was supplied but is not published

The shared service-hall photograph (clients seated at a counter, an Irembo-branded
desk, bank signage, and a large advertising banner for another company) is **not**
used on the site, and is deliberately not stored in this repository.

Three reasons:

1. **Third-party branding.** Another company's advertisement occupies a large part
   of the frame. Publishing it on R&M's website would suggest an affiliation that
   has not been verified.
2. **People who did not consent.** Members of the public are clearly identifiable.
   Publishing their faces on a commercial website needs their permission.
3. **Premises.** The photograph would imply R&M operates premises it may share
   rather than own.

To publish it later, R&M needs to confirm permission to use the location, consent
from the people shown, and a crop that removes the other company's advertisement.

## Replacing or adding photographs

Keep the dimensions recorded in `content/site.ts` → `photos` in step with the
files. Those values reserve each image's space in the layout, so a mismatch
causes visible layout shift while the page loads.
